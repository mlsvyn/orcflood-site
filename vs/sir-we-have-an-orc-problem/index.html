Sir, We Have an Orc Problem — on mobile?

Last updated: August 14, 2026 · Written by the developer of Orc Flood, an independent game not affiliated with Sir, We Have an Orc Problem or Mumpitz Games.

Is there a mobile version of Sir, We Have an Orc Problem?

No. Sir, We Have an Orc Problem by Mumpitz Games is available on Steam for PC only. No official iOS or Android release exists, and if you're after the same experience on a phone, the closest thing is an independently made game in the same genre — more on that below.

Is it on iOS or Android at all? What about APK downloads?

It is not on the App Store or Google Play. Be careful with sites offering an "APK" or "mobile download" of it: since no official mobile build exists, those files are at best fakes and at worst malware. If a mobile port is ever announced, it will be on the developer's own channels first.

What's the closest mobile game to it?

That's the game this site is about. Orc Flood: Tower Defense was built for exactly this itch on iOS and Android: the horde is one continuous simulated tide (up to 160 000 orcs in the final campaign) instead of discrete waves. You place up to 28 towers and aim every barrel yourself. It is an original, unaffiliated game, not a port: our own engine, art, levels and balance, made for touchscreens from the start.

Does Orc Flood work offline? Ads? Timers?

Fully offline, no ads, no analytics, no accounts. Free to start, with a single one-time unlock for the full campaign. No energy meters, no timers, no chests. Losses pay out scrap that funds your next attempt, and the keep remembers your tower layout per level.
