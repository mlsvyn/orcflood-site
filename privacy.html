Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026 · Applies to the mobile game Orc Flood: Tower Defense on Google Play and the App Store.

The short version

Orc Flood collects no personal data. None. The game runs entirely on your device, requires no account, and sends nothing anywhere.

Data we collect

  • Personal information: none collected, stored, or shared.
  • Analytics and tracking: none. The game contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers.
  • Network access: the game is fully playable offline and makes no network requests of its own.

Game saves

Your progress (campaign saves, settings) is stored locally on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no copy of it and no way to access it.

In-app purchases

The optional full-game unlock is processed entirely by the platform store — Google Play or the App Store. We never see or store your payment details, name, or address. The stores' own privacy policies apply to payment processing: Google, Apple.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, no data is collected from children either.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated first and the effective date revised.
